# screenshot_2025-12-23.png — OPS animal definitions
**Group B · screenshot of an OPS video · confidence: high**

A stitched wide capture of a slide. Left: a 2×2 of the four attitudes —
**Di** (Fi or Ti) · **Oe** (Se or Ne) · **Oi** (Si or Ni) · **De** (Fe or Te) — with arcs joining
each pair, labelled C, S, B, P. Right, the table verbatim:

| Animal | | Composition | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sleep** | I I | (Di + Oi) | Preserving energy, processing, introspective |
| **Consume** | I E | (Di + Oe) | Learning, taking in more, respect info, filling |
| **Blast** | E I | (De + Oi) | Teaching, controlling, getting started, bragging |
| **Play** | E E | (De + Oe) | Expending energy, work, doing, showing off |

## Why this one matters

**This is direct confirmation, from the owner's own saved material, of the animal correction made
to the engine.** The retired Python reference had Play and Consume transposed. The corrected
mapping in `src/engine/ops.ts` is:

```
Play    = Oe + De   energy
Sleep   = Oi + Di   energy
Blast   = Oi + De   information
Consume = Oe + Di   information
```

which is exactly what this slide shows. The old table had Play = Oe+Di and Consume = Oe+De, i.e.
Play and Consume swapped — mislabelling the primary animal of all sixteen types.

The correction was originally made against the published OPS definitions found by web research;
this image is an independent second source for the same fact, and it was sitting in the batch the
whole time.
