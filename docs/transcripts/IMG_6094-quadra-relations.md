A fourth 16×16 relations table, Socionics notation, grouped by quadra membership:

- **Inner-Quadra:** (Id) Identity · (Mr) Mirror · (Ac) Activity · (Dl) Duality
- **Outer-Quadra:** (Ki) **Kindred** · (Pd) Partial-Duality · (Ex) Extinguishment · (Se) Super-ego ·
  (Bu) **Business** · (Mg) Mirage · (Qi) Quasi-Identity · (Co) Conflicting
- **Asymmetric:** (S▲) Supervisor · (S▼) Supervisee · (B▲) Benefactor · (B▼) Beneficiary,
  read as "the type in the top column precedes the left column as…"

## Why this one is useful

It settles the naming question the other two charts raised. `IMG_6095` called the same two
relations *Look-a-like* and *Comparative*; `IMG_6097` called them *Cooperation* and *Congenerity*.
**This sheet calls them Kindred and Business — which is exactly what this app calls them.**

So of three published charts covering the same structure, one matches the app's naming and two use
different labels. That confirms the divergence is a genuine inconsistency in English-language
Socionics sources rather than an error in the app, and that the app's choice is well-supported.
The lexicon should record the aliases.

The grouping itself is also a nice observation the app does not make explicit: four of the sixteen
relations occur only *within* a quadra, and the rest only across quadras.
