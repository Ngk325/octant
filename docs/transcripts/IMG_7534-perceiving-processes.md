# IMG_7534 — "Essential Characteristics of the Perceiving Processes"
**Group C · photographed printed handout (Linda Berens / Interstrata house style) · confidence: high**

Four quadrants (Se, Ne, Si, Ni). Each carries five verb-phrases, a bulleted characteristic
list, and two catchphrases. Handwritten annotations in blue ink map each to the Function Roles
of IMG_7533 ("Doing", "Creating", "Preserving", "Knowing").

## Extraverted Sensing (Se)
Verbs: Experiencing · Doing · Observing and Responding · Adapting and Varying · **Present**
Characteristics: Current perceptions vividly capturing attention · Paying attention to what stands
out and is impactful · Becoming aware of rich sensory details · Noticing what's happening "now" as
it changes · Scanning the current situation for relevant information · Energy going to more and new
stimulation · Focusing on possibilities for action · Talking about things to do, actions to take ·
Asking for specific details to perceive the pattern · Reading minimal nonverbal cues · Seeking
aesthetic purity and pleasure in experiences · Attention turning outward to more sensory input ·
Living an experience
Catchphrases: *"This is what is." / "What's next?"*

## Extraverted iNtuiting (Ne)
Verbs: Inferring · Hypothesizing · Seeing Potentials · Wondering and Brainstorming · **Emergent**
Characteristics: Current perceptions sparking alternatives · Paying attention to relationships and
connections · Becoming aware of patterns, implications, and meanings · Noticing meta-communications
and what is not said · Scanning the current situation for what might possibly be · Energy going to
interactions to generate more possibilities · Focusing on multiple aspects of the whole context ·
Talking about possibilities, new ideas, meanings · Asking, "Have you thought about…?" · Reading the
meanings of a situation · Seeking more possibilities, ideas, options · Attention turning outward to
more relationships and meanings · Interpreting an experience
Catchphrases: *"This is what might be." / "It could be this, or this, or this, or…"*

## Introverted Sensing (Si)
Verbs: Recalling · Linking · Comparing and Contrasting · Noticing Match and Mismatch · **Past**
Characteristics: Current perceptions eliciting stored impressions · Paying attention to similarities
and differences · Becoming aware of differences from what was · Noticing discrepancies · Scanning
memory bank for related information · Energy staying with the recalled image · Focusing on past
successes (or failures) · Talking about past experiences · Asking for history or prior experience ·
Reading lessons from the past · Seeking to avoid mistakes made before · Attention turning inward to
images of past impressions · Re-living an experience
Catchphrases: *"This is how it has always been." / "This reminds me of…"*

## Introverted iNtuiting (Ni)
Verbs: Foreseeing · Conceptualizing · Understanding Complex Patterns · Synthesizing and Symbolizing · **Future**
Characteristics: Current perceptions sparking insights into complex situations · Paying attention to
future implications · Becoming aware of universal meanings and symbols · Noticing whole patterns or
systems · Scanning internal images for insights · Energy staying with the vision · Focusing on depth
of understanding · Talking about the future and the meaning · Asking, "What is the goal?" · Reading
the future and the potential in others · Seeking innovative ideas or universal symbols · Attention
turning inward to images forming of the future · Imagining and anticipating an experience
Catchphrases: *"This is how it will be." / "Aha, that's it!"*
