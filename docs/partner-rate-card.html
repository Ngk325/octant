Recorded lessons

Recording converts a recurring cost — our hours, every time a new person needs the model — into a one-time cost plus a durable asset. The only real question is who funds it and what they get for it.

Option 1 — Octant funds it

Videos sit behind the standard wall and are included with every seat under any shape, at no extra cost to you. Our consultancy hours fall toward zero as the library fills.

Timeline is ours to set and is not currently committed.

Option 2 — You fund it

A course-length set runs $8,000–15,000 to produce, depending on length and how much is bespoke to your context.

In exchange: category exclusivity for twelve months — no other partner in your vertical — and your framing baked into the material rather than bolted on after.

Option 2 is the one worth thinking about if your offering’s real bottleneck is that your clients need to understand the model, not just consult it.

What we need to quote you

Four answers turn everything above into a single number with your name on it.

  1. Which shape — A, B, C or D. If none cuts in the right place, say so; a fifth shape is available.
  2. Roughly how many people would hold a seat in year one, and whether that is your staff, your clients, or both.
  3. Who your client thinks they are buying from at renewal. That single answer separates A from B, C and D more cleanly than any feature comparison.
  4. When you would want to launch. A and B need nothing built; C and D need a scoping conversation before a date means anything.

Terms that apply to all four

  • Everything on both pages is fixed until 18 October 2026. If a number changes after that, you get the changed number before you are asked to decide anything.
  • Invoicing is monthly or annually in advance depending on the tier; annual tiers are invoiced once, at the start of the term.
  • Seat counts are trued up quarterly under B, C and D. You are never billed for a seat before it exists.
  • The engine, the course, the lexicon and the assistant are included under every shape. There are no feature gates and no per-report fees at any tier.
One term that is not negotiable at any price. Octant is not a validated psychometric instrument and does not claim to be. It must not be used as a hiring, selection, promotion or termination instrument, and any integration will say so where users can see it. We would rather lose the deal than have this one bend — it is the same reason the product ships the survey evidence that disagrees with it.
