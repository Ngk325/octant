# Octant — the vocabulary, locked

Every term the product uses, with one final name each. No alternatives,
no "or". Where the earlier proposal offered a choice, the choice is made
here and the reason is given.

This is the spec the implementation follows. If a word is not in this
file, it is not in the product.

**Status:** approved, not yet implemented. The app still ships the old
terms; see `§9 Migration`.

---

## 1. Kept as-is

Not everything changes. These stay exactly as they are, because renaming
them costs more than it wins.

| Term | Why it stays |
|---|---|
| `Ne Ni Se Si Te Ti Fe Fi` | The interchange format. How readers arrive, what they search, what the calculator resolves to. |
| `ENTP`, `INFJ`, … | The primary key. Every route, share link and outside conversation. |
| Observer / Decider | Already plain English, already working. |
| Gate of Chaos / Obligation / the Tribe / the Self | Derived here, named here. Nothing to replace. |
| Joy · Decay · Hope · Despair | Ordinary words, already ours in all but arrangement. |
| Ease | Plain, unambiguous, not borrowed. |
| Complement · Catalyst | Our own framing for "restful" vs "stimulating". Keep both. |
| Quadra colours, the four hue families | Semantic, tested, unchanged. |

## 2. The stack

### The eight slots

| # | New | Was | One line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lead** | Hero | Strongest, runs by default, invisible to its owner. |
| 2 | **Support** | Parent | Carries the lead and makes it usable by others. |
| 3 | **Delight** | Child | Strong enough to enjoy, not to rely on. |
| 4 | **Cave** | Inferior | The weak point carrying the growth. |
| 5 | **Doubt** | Nemesis | The internal opposition, arriving as anxiety. |
| 6 | **Scold** | Critic | The support function turned inward as judgement. |
| 7 | **Blind spot** | Trickster | Confident output that is simply wrong. |
| 8 | **Dread** | Demon | Surfaces under threat; destructive and briefly effective. |

Groups: **the front four** (1–4) and **the back four** (5–8). Never
"top/bottom" — that implies better and worse, which the model denies.

> `Cave` and `Delight` are already in the app's own prose, and
> `gate.cave` is a real engine field. This promotes words we reached for
> anyway.
>
> `Dread` also settles a live collision: *demon* currently means both
> slot 8 and an overlay function, in different blocks, with nothing on
> screen to say which.

### The four sides

| New | Was | One line |
|---|---|---|
| **Front** | Ego | The side you run by default. |
| **Reach** | Subconscious | The side you are reaching for. Structurally your Counterpart. |
| **Reserve** | Unconscious | What runs when the Front is exhausted. |
| **Guard** | Superego | What you perform under threat. |

Collective: **the four sides** (unchanged — plain, and ours).

### The doors

Each side has exactly one **door**, and the function that opens it is its
**key**. The four doors, in the order they open:

| Door | Key | State | Blocked by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front | Lead | open — you live here | — |
| Reach | Cave | ajar | insecurity |
| Reserve | Doubt | closed | worry |
| Guard | Dread | barred | fear |

**Forced opening** — what happens when a door stays shut too long and
gets opened for you rather than by you.

| New | Was |
|---|---|
| **First reckoning** | midlife crisis (the Reach door, forced) |
| **Second reckoning** | three-quarter-life crisis (the Reserve door, forced) |

> "Midlife crisis" is a clinical-sounding borrowed phrase for something
> the model treats structurally. *Reckoning* keeps the weight and drops
> the diagnosis.

## 7. Structure terms

| New | Was |
|---|---|
| **Stack map** | Model A |
| **the three moves** | the three involutions |
| **flip** | α (alpha) — flip the attitude |
| **swap** | β (beta) — swap the element |
| **turn** | ω (omega) — do both |

> The Greek letters are the single most confusing thing in the lexicon.
> `α · flip attitude` asks a reader to learn a symbol *and* a definition
> to understand one idea they could have had directly. The operations are
> named for what they do; the symbols stay in code comments only.

## 9. Migration

1. **Codes are stable.** Relation codes, function codes and type codes do
   not change, so no derivation, score, test fixture or route moves.
2. **Rename in three passes**, smallest blast radius first:
   - display labels only — romance, temperaments, interaction styles;
   - relations and the overlay;
   - the stack, the sides, the Rose.
3. **The plain glosses are the real work.** Every term carries a
   plain-language line written around the current word. There are more of
   them than there are terms and they are harder to write. Budget for
   that, not for the renaming.
4. **Carry a forwarding address for one release.** Anyone who has used
   the app knows "Dual" and "savior". A quiet parenthetical in each
   lexicon entry — not attribution, a redirect — then drop it.

---

## 10. Where other systems' names are allowed

Exactly one module: **`src/engine/translation.ts`**, plus the view that
renders it and the per-type "known elsewhere as" row that reads from it.

`tests/attribution.test.ts` keeps failing the build on those names
everywhere else in `src/` — including the type reader's own copy, the
lexicon, the curriculum and the assistant's primer. The allowlist is one
file, not a policy.

The translation data itself, and the four places the mapping genuinely
breaks, are specified in the v4 translation key.
